The Parent and Family Guide

Getting your child from signed up to playing music, with completely free lessons every step of the way.

Families  ·  theresonancefoundation.org
01
Getting Started

Add Your Students

One parent login manages your whole household. Start by adding each child you would like enrolled.

  1. Sign in and open My Students in the My Family section of the sidebar.
  2. Click Add student and share their name, the instrument they want to learn, and their current level. Total beginners are very welcome.
  3. New students start as Pending while your chapter reviews the registration. You will get an email and a portal notification the moment they are approved.
Lessons are free

Every lesson is completely free. Group and individual lessons both cost nothing, always. If anything ever appears to ask you for payment for a lesson, something is wrong; please tell us.

02
Scheduling

Request Lessons

Once a student is approved and matched with a tutor, the Lessons page is where scheduling happens.

  1. Click Request a lesson.
  2. Pick your student, their tutor, one of the tutor's weekly availability slots, and the date you want.
  3. Add a note if there is anything the tutor should know, and send the request.
  4. The tutor confirms, and the lesson lands on your calendar. If a time stops working, cancel the pending request or message the tutor to rearrange.
03
Between Lessons

Practice and Assignments

Two small habits make lessons dramatically better: logging practice and checking assignments.

  • Practice Log: tap Log practice after your child plays and record the minutes and what they worked on. Their tutor sees it and shapes the next lesson around it, and students love watching their streak grow.
  • Assignments: homework the tutor leaves after each lesson shows up here instantly, so nothing gets lost between sessions.

Everything else in your sidebar

  • Messages reaches your child's tutor and chapter staff directly. Keep lesson coordination in the portal so there is always a record, and skip trading personal numbers.
  • Calendar shows lessons and chapter events in one view.
  • Events lists recitals and community performances. RSVP when your family plans to come; organizers count on it.
  • Announcements and Resources carry chapter news and useful files like the practice log template. Important updates also arrive by email.
Safety

Portal messages involving students can be reviewed by chapter leadership when there is a concern. It is one of the ways we keep every conversation appropriate and every student safe.

Questions or something broken? Email administrator@theresonancefoundation.org and a real person will get back to you.
