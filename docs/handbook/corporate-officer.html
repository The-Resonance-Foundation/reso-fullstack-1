The Corporate Officer Guide

How to run organization wide events and stay connected in the Resonance Foundation member portal.

Corporate Officers  ·  theresonancefoundation.org
01
Your Role

What You Do Here

Corporate officers work at the organization level. Your job in the portal is organization wide events: creating them, keeping their details current, and watching who is coming.

Sign in at theresonancefoundation.org and you land on your dashboard. Your sidebar keeps things simple: Dashboard, Messages, Announcements, Calendar, Events, and Resources. The dashboard greets you with upcoming events and quick actions into the pages you use most.

Scope

You operate at the corporate level only. Chapter internals (students, families, applicants, tutors) and donation records are outside your view by design. If you need something from a chapter, message its leadership.

02
Your Main Tool

Managing Events

The Events page is your workspace. It lists every upcoming event with its chapter tag, location, time, status, and your own RSVP state.

Creating an organization wide event

  1. Open Events and click Create event.
  2. Leave the chapter set to Organization-wide. That is your lane; chapter events are created by chapter officers and presidents.
  3. Give it a clear title, a short description people will actually read, and the venue.
  4. Pick the start and end date and time. Add a capacity if seats are limited.
  5. Choose the status. Published goes live for everyone immediately; draft stays hidden while you finish planning.

After it is live

  • Members RSVP from the same page, and the counts update live. Check them before booking chairs or printing programs.
  • You can edit details any time. RSVPs close on their own once the event has passed.
  • After the event, attendance tracking feeds volunteer hour records for the people who helped.
03
Day To Day

Staying Connected

Three quiet pages keep you in the loop between events.

  • Calendar lays out everything you can access by month or week, so you can spot conflicts before you schedule a showcase on top of a recital.
  • Announcements is the organization bulletin board. New posts also reach your email.
  • Messages connects you with staff and chapter leadership. Keep event coordination in here so there is always a record.

Appointments

Corporate officers are appointed and removed by the Board of Directors alone. If your team is changing, that conversation goes to the board.
