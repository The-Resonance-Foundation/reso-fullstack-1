First Things First

Set Your Availability

Everything starts with the Availability page. Families can only request lessons inside the windows you publish, so an accurate week means requests you can actually say yes to.

  1. Open Availability in the Teaching section of the sidebar.
  2. Click Add slot and pick the day, start time, and end time you can teach.
  3. Add as many windows as you like, and remove one with its x whenever your schedule changes.
Lesson requests

When a family requests one of your slots, you get a notification and the request appears in your queue. Accept it and the lesson is created for both sides, or decline with a short note so the family can pick another time. Answer quickly; families are waiting on you.

02
Teaching

Your Students

My Students lists everyone assigned to you, with their instrument and level. Each student has a hub where all of your teaching tools live.

Open a hub and you can do everything for that student in one place:

  • Schedule lessons and log attendance or notes after each one. That running record makes every next lesson better.
  • Assign homework with New assignment. The family sees it instantly, so nothing gets lost between sessions.
  • Check practice in the read only practice view, which shows what the family logs at home.
  • Share resources like sheet music or exercises directly with that student.
03
Your Service Counts

Hours and Certificates

Tutoring with the Foundation is volunteer service. Log it, get it approved, and receive an official certificate you can use for school requirements and college applications.

  1. Open Log Hours, pick your chapter and the Teaching category (or event support or admin work when that fits better).
  2. Enter the hours, the date it happened, and a sentence about what you did.
  3. Submit. Your chapter president or a program administrator reviews it. While it is pending you can still edit or delete the entry.
  4. The moment your hours are approved, an official certificate PDF is generated with your name, total hours, and the approver's signature. Find it any time under Certificates, ready to preview or download.
Good to know

If an entry is rejected, you get the reason and can log a corrected one. Nobody can approve their own hours, and certificates never expire or disappear from your account.

The rest of your toolkit

  • Messages reaches your students' families and your chapter leadership. Keep lesson coordination in the portal.
  • Calendar shows your lessons and chapter events in one view.
  • Events lists performances and gatherings; RSVP when you plan to come or help.
  • Announcements and Resources keep you up to date, with everything also arriving by email.
